Intelligent Freight Forecasting & Vessel Chartering Decision Support System

SIH26006 | Ministry of Steel | Smart India Hackathon 2026

AI-assisted decision support for overseas bulk raw-material procurement and vessel chartering to India's East Coast.

DEMO DATA — NOT REAL MARKET DATA. This prototype returns clearly-labelled synthetic values so the decision workflow can be reviewed. The freight-forecasting and optimisation models are not implemented yet, and no real Indian freight-rate data is used.
About this system

A decision-support tool for Indian steel plants and PSUs importing bulk raw materials — coking coal, iron ore, limestone — from overseas to ports on the East Coast of India.

For a given procurement scenario it searches:

Overseas Origin × Cargo Type × East Coast Indian Port × Vessel Type × Chartering Week
→ lowest expected-cost feasible strategy

Intended users:

  • Steel-plant procurement managers
  • Logistics planners
  • PSU procurement / logistics decision makers

Feasible origin–cargo–port combinations come from the route dataset — not every origin supplies every cargo.
