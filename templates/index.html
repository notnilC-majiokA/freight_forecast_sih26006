Intelligent Freight Forecasting & Vessel Chartering Decision Support System

SIH26006 | Ministry of Steel | Smart India Hackathon 2026

India-centric decision support for overseas industrial bulk raw-material procurement and vessel chartering to the East Coast of India — what to charter, where from/to, when to enter the market, how to contract it, and why.

Prototype uses synthetic/demo market and infrastructure data. Production deployment would integrate validated historical, port and licensed market data. Forecasts demonstrate the architecture, not real market accuracy; risk and feasibility scores are model-based heuristic indicators.
From reactive spot buying to an actionable plan

This prototype walks one procurement scenario through:

  1. Predictive freight forecasting (Module A)
  2. Optimal market-entry timing (Module A)
  3. Feasible vessel-type optimization (Module B)
  4. Origin + destination port feasibility (Modules C/D)
  5. Spot vs short/medium-term MVC comparison (Module E)
  6. Idle-time management (Module F)
  7. Risk mitigation (Module G)
  8. Actionable chartering recommendation

Prototype commodity scope is intentionally limited to coking coal, iron ore and limestone. Feasible origin–cargo–port combinations come from data/routes.csv.
